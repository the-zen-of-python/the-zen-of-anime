# Writing and editorial guidelines

Thank you for helping build The Zen of Anime.

## Article principles

- Write in clear, accessible language.
- Use original wording; do not reproduce scripts or lengthy dialogue.
- Keep episode summaries concise.
- Make the distinction between what happens and what you think it means clear.
- Back interpretations with details from the episode.
- Mark spoiler scope near the beginning of each article.
- Avoid presenting theories as confirmed facts.
- Use respectful, constructive criticism.

## Episode article structure

1. Episode overview
2. Concise summary
3. Story structure
4. Character analysis
5. Conflict and stakes
6. Themes and symbolism
7. Visual and audio storytelling
8. Foreshadowing and narrative questions
9. What makes the episode work?
10. Lessons for storytellers
11. Final assessment
12. Discussion questions

Not every episode needs equal coverage of every section. Adapt the analysis to the episode.

## File naming

Use lowercase words separated by hyphens, for example:

`docs/one-piece/episodes/episode-001-romance-dawn.md`

## Spoiler policy

Use `episode-only` when the analysis should not reveal information beyond the episode being discussed. If later events are discussed, label the article clearly as containing series spoilers.
