# Conflict and Stakes

Conflict arises when a character's goal meets resistance. Stakes describe what matters about the outcome.

Useful questions:

- What does the character want right now?
- Who or what stands in the way?
- Why can't the problem be ignored?
- What happens if the character fails?
- How does the pressure increase or change?

A fight is not automatically compelling because it is visually spectacular. It becomes dramatically meaningful when the audience understands what the characters want and what the outcome means.
