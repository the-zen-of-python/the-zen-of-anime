# Character Arcs

A character arc is the meaningful change—or deliberate refusal to change—that a character experiences over a story.

Questions to ask:

- What does the character believe at the beginning?
- What experiences challenge that belief?
- Which choices reveal whether they are changing?
- What does the change cost them?
- Does the ending resolve, complicate, or reject the expected transformation?

Avoid assuming that every protagonist must change. A stable character can drive a story by changing the people and world around them.
