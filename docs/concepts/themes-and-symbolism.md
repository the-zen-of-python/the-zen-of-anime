# Themes and Symbolism

A theme is an idea or question explored by a story. A symbol is a detail that carries meaning beyond its literal role.

When proposing an interpretation:

1. Identify the moment or detail.
2. Explain the possible meaning.
3. Connect it to character, conflict, or repeated patterns.
4. Consider alternative readings.
5. Avoid presenting an interpretation as the creator's intention unless reliable evidence supports that claim.
