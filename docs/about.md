# About The Zen of Anime

**The Zen of Anime** is an independent project about looking more closely at anime stories.

The aim is not simply to recap episodes or rate them. We want to understand how stories work: how a character's decision creates conflict, how an episode earns an emotional moment, how a fictional world is revealed, and how visual choices shape an audience's experience.

## What you'll find

- Episode summaries and close readings
- Character studies and relationship analysis
- Essays on plot, pacing, themes, and symbolism
- Visual storytelling observations
- Practical lessons for writers and filmmakers

## Independent publication

The Zen of Anime is an unofficial, independent commentary project. Anime titles, characters, artwork, music, and other original materials belong to their respective rights holders. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators, studios, or distributors of the featured series.
