# The Zen of Anime

*Beyond the story. Into its meaning.*

Welcome to a thoughtful exploration of anime storytelling: the choices that shape characters, build worlds, create conflict, and give stories meaning.

## Explore the series

- **[One Piece](one-piece/index.md)** — ambition, adventure, world-building, and long-form storytelling.
- **[Jujutsu Kaisen](jujutsu-kaisen/index.md)** — conflict, tension, supernatural rules, and character motivation.
- **[Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood](fullmetal-alchemist-brotherhood/index.md)** — plot, moral dilemmas, consequences, and thematic depth.
- **[Naruto](naruto/index.md)** — character development, rivalry, belonging, and emotional storytelling.

## Our approach

Each article separates **what happens** from **how we interpret it**. We look at story structure, character choices, themes, and relevant visual or audio techniques, while keeping summaries concise and the analysis original.
