# The Zen of Anime

**Beyond the story. Into its meaning.**

The Zen of Anime is an independent publication exploring anime through episode summaries, character studies, narrative analysis, themes, symbolism, and visual storytelling.

## Featured series

- [One Piece](docs/one-piece/index.md) — adventure, ambition, world-building, and long-form storytelling
- [Jujutsu Kaisen](docs/jujutsu-kaisen/index.md) — conflict, tension, supernatural rules, and character motivation
- [Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood](docs/fullmetal-alchemist-brotherhood/index.md) — plot construction, moral dilemmas, and thematic depth
- [Naruto](docs/naruto/index.md) — character arcs, rivalry, belonging, and emotional storytelling

## What we explore

- Episode summaries written in original language
- Story structure, turning points, conflict, and stakes
- Character motivations, choices, and relationships
- Themes, symbolism, and subtext
- Direction, framing, editing, music, and pacing
- Practical lessons for writers and storytellers

## Editorial principles

1. Separate episode facts from interpretation.
2. Support analysis with specific moments from the episode.
3. Label spoilers clearly and avoid revealing later events in episode-only articles.
4. Distinguish evidence from speculation.
5. Keep summaries concise and focus on original commentary and criticism.
6. Respect the creators and rights holders of the works discussed.
